Jan D. Goldman

Admitted to the New York bar in October 1982, and to the Southern and Eastern District federal courts in the same year. Practising from Garden City ever since.

A small office, on purpose

The Law Offices of Jan D. Goldman and Associates represents individuals and business clients across New York in traffic violations, driving while intoxicated, criminal matters and negligence.

It is deliberately not a volume operation. Cases of this kind are decided by careful, ordinary work: reading the accusatory instrument properly, knowing the court and the prosecutor in front of you, preparing the defence that exists rather than the one a client would prefer, and saying early which outcomes are realistic.

You deal with the attorney. That is the whole argument for a small office.

Calls are returned. Cases are reported on while they are moving, not when they end.

What four decades buys you

Length of practice is not a claim about results, and nobody should read it as one. What it does mean is that very little about the process will be unfamiliar, and that you are not paying for somebody to learn a courthouse on your matter.

How you will be dealt with

You will be told what you are charged with in plain English, what the exposure is, and what the likely paths are. If the honest answer is unwelcome, you will still be given it.
