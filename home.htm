What a conviction actually costs you

Points on your license

Points count from the date of the violation, and they stay on your total for 24 months. Reach 11 in that window and your license may be suspended.

  • Passing a stopped school bus8

The Driver Responsibility Assessment

Reach six points in eighteen months and the DMV charges a separate assessment on top of every fine and surcharge the court imposes. It is billed annually for three years, and your license is suspended if it goes unpaid.

This is a DMV charge, not a court fine, so it arrives after you think the matter is closed.

Your insurance

Insurers are permitted to raise premiums after a traffic conviction, and the increase can follow you for years after the ticket is paid. For many drivers this costs more than the fine ever did.

A plea that keeps points off your record is often worth more than the fine you avoid.

Point values and the 24 month lookback are taken from the New York State Department of Motor Vehicles driver point system. Figures are current as of the date on this page and are given as general information, not as legal advice about your case.
