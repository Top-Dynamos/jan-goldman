What to have ready

A first call goes much further when the paperwork is in front of you. Most of what matters is printed on the ticket itself.

  • The charge, and the section number printed beside it
  • The date of the violation, which is the date the points attach to
  • The court named on the ticket, and any date you have been given
  • Whether you have other tickets or convictions inside the last two years
  • For a collision, any photographs, the police report number and your insurer's details
